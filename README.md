### About Me

I am an **MSc student in Computer Science — Soft Computing and Artificial Intelligence** at the **University of Tehran** and a Computer Science graduate from the **University of Bojnord**. My work bridges deep learning research with production-ready backend engineering.

- **Research Interests:** Computer Vision, Medical Imaging, Vision-Language Models (VLMs), and Agentic AI
- **Currently Working On:** [Medical AI Platform](https://github.com/mjavadzare/medical-ai-platform) — a modular deep learning platform for clinical imaging (diabetic retinopathy detection)
- **Leadership:** Former Executive Board Member at the Computer Science Scientific Association, University of Bojnord (Co-organizer & web developer for UBPC contests)
- **Portfolio & Full CV:** [mjavadzare.github.io](https://mjavadzare.github.io/)
